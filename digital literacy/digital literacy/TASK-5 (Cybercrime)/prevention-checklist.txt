Stay safe online
  
1. Use strong, unique passwords
2. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
3. Never click on unknown links
4. Keep your software and apps updated
5. Use only trusted Wi-Fi networks
6. Review app permissions regularly
UPI and Financial safety
7. A UPI PIN is for sending money, never receiving it
8. Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, or card CVV numbers with anyone
